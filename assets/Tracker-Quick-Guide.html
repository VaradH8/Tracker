Role 01 · Signs in to → My Day

The Lead
Own it. Sign it off.

You own delivery on your projects: set the plan, shape the team, watch health — and give the final ✓ before work is truly done.

Your one big job — sign-off
Task marked Done→ “Needs sign-off” banner→ Approve ✓ or Send back with a remark
Doers can never approve their own work. Every approval is logged with your name and time.
Your day, in 5 moves
01
Open My Day
Scan the cards: due today, overdue, blocked, and idle team-mates.
02
Jump into a slipping project
Check its board by week; open the History tab to see who changed what.
03
Approve Done tasks
Or send them back with a remark — you are the quality gate.
04
Adjust the plan
Change roster or dates; the team is notified automatically.
05
Clear Leaves
Approve or deny pending leave requests.
Can & can't

You can

  • Create projects & tasks; manage the team roster
  • Sign off (or revoke) Done work on your projects
  • See financials (hours vs. budget) & full history
  • Approve leave · export data · review Resources

You can't

  • Day-to-day task edits — that's the Co-ordinator
  • Import tasks from spreadsheets
  • Manage users or system settings (Admin only)

Role 02 · Signs in to → My Day

The Co-ordinator
Make the week happen.

You're the engine room. Assign work, move dates, unblock people, keep every board current — you have the fullest hands-on control of any role.

The board you run — every task moves like this
To Do→ In Progress→ In review→ Done ✓
Blocked can happen from In Progress (a reason is captured) — it lands on your My Day instantly. Anything not Done carries forward to next week automatically.
Your day, in 5 moves
01
Open My Day
Due today · overdue · blocked · idle — your whole team at a glance.
02
Clear the overdue pile — in bulk
Tick several tasks → reassign or set a new date in one go. The list only offers people with the same role as the current owner.
03
Keep boards honest
Add missing tasks, set priorities, assign developers — you can edit any field on any task in your projects.
04
Unblock people
Chase the dependency or the person; flip the task back to In Progress.
05
Star what matters ⭐
The Important star pins a task to the org-wide Dashboard for leadership.
Can & can't

You can

  • Edit everything on any task: priority, dates, assignees, status, dependencies, star ⭐
  • Bulk reassign / re-date from My Day
  • Import a spreadsheet of tasks into a project
  • Sign off Done work · approve leave · export

You can't

  • Manage users or system settings (Admin only)
  • That's it — everything else is yours

Role 03 · Signs in to → Pipeline

The Business Developer
Win it. Hand it off.

You live at the front of the funnel: track deals, keep the client directory fresh, and turn every win into a project for delivery to run.

Your board — drag deals across four stages
Lead→ Quoted→ Won→ Kicked off
Each deal shows value, win probability & expected start; the header sums the weighted open value (value × probability).
Your day, in 4 moves
01
Open the Pipeline
Drag deals between stages; add a New deal or drop a dead one.
02
Deal won? Kick off the project
Create the project, pick the client (or add a new one), and name its Lead + Co-ordinator.
03
Keep Clients fresh
Contacts, industry, and each client's list of projects.
04
Hand off & watch
Delivery staffs the project and runs the tasks; you keep sight of financials and status.
Can & can't

You can

  • Manage the Pipeline — add, drag & drop deals
  • Add clients & keep the directory current
  • Create a project and name its Lead + Co-ordinator
  • Create first tasks on a project you kicked off
  • See financials on your projects · export data

You can't

  • Manage a project's team roster or delivery details
  • Edit task fields, assign developers, or sign off
  • See audit history · import tasks · manage users

Role 04 · Signs in to → My Tasks

The Developer
One click at a time.

Your world is simple: the tasks assigned to you, one button to move each forward, and a timer that logs your effort as you go.

Every task — buttons on the card do it all
To Do— Start → In Progress— Review → In review→ Done ✓
Stuck? Hit Block + a reason — your Co-ordinator sees it immediately; Unblock returns it. Start also runs the timer; Done stops it and sends the task for sign-off.
Your day, in 5 moves
01
Open My Tasks in Focus mode
Only what needs attention now: due, overdue, in progress. “Show all” reveals the backlog.
02
Hit Start on the top task
Timer runs, status flips to In Progress. Stop pauses; resume any time — even days later.
03
Stuck? Block it with a reason
Don't sit on it quietly — a blocked task gets help fast.
04
Finished? Click Done
Timer stops; the task goes to a Lead/Co-ordinator for sign-off.
05
Check Notifications
Reply to @mentions; type @ in a remark to loop someone in.
Can & can't

You can

  • Update status & run the timer on your own tasks
  • Add remarks, attachments & @mention colleagues
  • Self-assign to tasks on projects you're on
  • Use the Calendar & request leave

You can't

  • Edit priority, dates or assignees on tasks · star ⭐
  • Create projects, manage rosters, or sign off work
  • See budgets, the pipeline, history, or exports
